In the small town of Red Hollow, a failing family pizzeria tries to revive business with a new "Build-Your-Nightmare Pizza Challenge." But the toppings customers choose begin appearing in horrifying ways around town—and the pizzas seem to be choosing their creators back.

Before embarking on his journey across Gaia, Vivi lived quietly in the marshes with his grandfather, Quan. While most people knew Quan as a skilled fisherman and a wise Qu, few knew that he spent his evenings teaching Vivi the art of making pizza.

Every morning, Quan would gather fresh ingredients from the marsh. He showed Vivi how to collect mushrooms, herbs, and vegetables while explaining that good food began with patience and care. Back at their small hut, Quan taught him how to knead dough, spread sauce evenly, and choose toppings that complemented one another.

At first, Vivi struggled. The dough would stick to his gloves, and toppings often ended up scattered across the table. Rather than becoming frustrated, Quan encouraged him to keep trying. He believed that mistakes were part of learning and that every pizza told a story.

As the seasons passed, Vivi's confidence grew. He learned how to shape perfect crusts and bake pizzas until they were golden brown. Travelers who wandered near Qu's Marsh were often surprised to find such delicious food in such a remote place. Many claimed there was something special about Vivi's cooking—a warmth that could brighten even the darkest day.

More importantly, Quan taught Vivi that making pizza was about more than preparing food. It was about sharing kindness with others and bringing people together. Those lessons remained with Vivi long after he left the marsh and began his adventure.

Though the world would come to know Vivi as a courageous Black Mage, the simple skills he learned beside his grandfather never faded. According to local legend, every great pizza recipe can be traced back to those peaceful evenings in Qu's Marsh, where a young mage learned that the best ingredients were patience, friendship, and a little bit of love.
